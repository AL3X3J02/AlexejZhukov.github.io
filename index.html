    Backstory

    I was born in Drogheda in Ireland, but I’ve lived in Cambridge for pretty much my whole life. I got into making things through video editing, effects and animation in Adobe software, and spent a lot of time messing around with cameras and computers. Over time that turned into programming, and then into making games.

    My undergrad dissertation was a remake of a classic arcade game in Unity with a few twists of my own. For my master’s I went bigger and simulated AI NPCs reacting to an apocalyptic event in Unreal Engine 5.

    Team play

    My favourite team experience so far was a 48 hour game jam. I led a team of three and we shipped Ha-Ha Haunters, a silly survival game where you fight off ghosts using animals as weapons, each with their own sounds and abilities.
